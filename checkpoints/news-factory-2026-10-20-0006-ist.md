### Google for Startups Cloud Program in India

- Current official eligibility/application check: 2026-10-20
- Source publication date: Not stated on the current official eligibility and FAQ pages
- Program framing: Ongoing program; not presented as a new launch
- Application status: Official eligibility and FAQ pages display Apply now when checked
- India finding: Google's official India startup page lists Google cloud credits for startups and provides a Join the program route
- Fixed application deadline: None stated on the official pages checked
- Start tier: Up to US$2,000 in Google Cloud credits, valid for one year; US$200 Google Skills credits
- Scale tier: Up to US$200,000 over two years; Year 1 is 100% up to US$100,000 and Year 2 is 20% up to an additional US$100,000
- AI-first Scale tier: Up to US$350,000; Year 1 is 100% up to US$250,000 and Year 2 follows the published 20% structure up to an additional US$100,000
- Important classification: Service credits, not cash, investment funding or a guaranteed award
- Start-tier criteria: Digital-native technology startup with working MVP, clear business model, fundraising plans, founded within 24 months and no prior Cloud credits beyond free trial
- Scale-tier criteria: Published funding/founding recency rules and no more than US$5,000 in prior Cloud credits
- Key exclusions: Educational institutions, dev shops, consultancies and agencies may not be accepted
- Application requirements include an 18-character billing account ID and a business email matching the startup website domain
- Google says many decisions arrive within a few business days, but manual review can take ten days or longer; this is not a guaranteed turnaround
- Official eligibility: https://cloud.google.com/startup/benefits
- Official FAQ: https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq
- Official India startup page: https://grow.google/intl/en_in/startups/
- Program overview: https://startup.google.com/cloud/
- India contracting-entity reference: https://cloud.google.com/terms/google-entity
