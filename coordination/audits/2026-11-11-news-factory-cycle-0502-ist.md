### New draft — VSSC Academic Project Work 2027 (Forsk #172)

The official VSSC HRDD portal currently lists two distinct project-work routes. The portal does not display a publication/last-updated date in the accessible view; the status below is a live portal snapshot checked 11 November 2026. The linked UG form also has no visible issue date in extracted text.

**Undergraduate route**
- Project period: 1 January–31 March 2027.
- Portal receipt cut-off: **15 November 2026**.
- Eligible courses in the official form: final-year BE/BTech; BSc Physics/Chemistry; Diploma Electronics/Mechanical/Computer Science.
- Indian citizen; BE/BTech must have completed sixth semester; BSc/Diploma must have completed fourth semester.
- Minimum aggregate: 60% or CGPA 6.32/10, with the marks range determined by course.
- Group applications: maximum four students, all from the same branch, semester and college.
- Duration: BE/BTech 45–90 days; BSc/Diploma 45–60 days.
- Prescribed form must be completed by student and college authority, with detailed biodata; email scanned form to o_hrdd@vssc.gov.in. The form says the completed application must reach VSSC before the cut-off.
- No stipend/remuneration/financial assistance. The UG form does not state a separate application-fee rule; the article does not claim the UG route is fee-free.
- Form: https://vsscinternship.vssc.gov.in/HRDD_LOGIN/UGDownloadForm.pdf

**Postgraduate / integrated route**
- Project period: 1 January–30 June 2027.
- Online application window: 15 October 2026, 10:00 AM to **16 November 2026, 11:59 PM**.
- Courses include ME/MTech, MSc and specified integrated ME/MTech/MSc/BS-MS. MSc Computer Science in Data Analytics and Data Science are explicitly listed.
- Minimum aggregate: 60% or CGPA 6.32/10; semester prerequisites apply (ME/MTech/MSc: first semester completed; integrated routes/BS-MS: seventh semester completed, marks through sixth semester).
- Duration: MSc/Integrated MSc/BS-MS 120–180 days; ME/MTech/Integrated ME/MTech 120–365 days.
- No stipend/remuneration/financial assistance. The official PG project-work instructions explicitly state no fee.
- Supporting information may be uploaded as one PDF up to 5 MB.
- Project work is restricted to unclassified areas; selection depends on facilities/project availability and VSSC may accept/reject applications.
- Portal: https://vsscinternship.vssc.gov.in/HRDD_LOGIN/beforelogincontroller
- Instructions: https://vsscinternship.vssc.gov.in/HRDD_LOGIN/instructionBeforLogin.jsp

**Separate internship intake — not the same application**
- INT 08 (1 November 2026–31 January 2027) is marked closed.
- Next INT 09 internship slot is 1 May–31 July 2027; its application window is 15 February–15 March 2027.
- The article explicitly prevents readers from confusing the project-work deadlines with the internship intake.
