## Link acquisition rules
1. Inventory all course landing pages with canonical, status, title, topic and target query.
2. Prioritize contextual internal links from relevant course-category, blog, syllabus and comparison pages.
3. Only publish cross-domain links where a genuine editorial comparison or useful complementary resource justifies them; label affiliations when applicable.
4. Do not create reciprocal sitewide link wheels, paid unmarked links, doorway pages or fabricated external backlinks.
5. Verify each published source URL, anchor text, destination status, indexability and resulting placement.
